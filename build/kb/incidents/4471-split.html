The three problems

Parser (parser). The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date whose day is 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped. Reports say “7 April went out on 4 July”. This is the fix #4471 has queued.

booking-v3 date hint (hint). booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them and get the swap. It only shows on the form channel at tenants that run booking-v3 (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). The parser fix does not touch it: the hint or the form has to change.

One-day move (one-day). Five reports are not a swap at all. The date moves by one day: “12 May” shows as “11 May”, “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, one meeting reached a client in Lisbon as Tuesday and a client in Toronto as Monday, and “every booking in June is one day off”. That points at time zones (an invite sent as local midnight read in another zone), not at date order. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json; the old table only footnoted them.

Why they ended up together

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and every date report has a subject like “Wrong day on the invite”. It accepted this risk; nobody split the ticket later.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser locale, not the tenant. An en-US browser at a day-first tenant gets tagged hint, a day-first browser on a booking-v3 form gets tagged parser. The “intake tag wrong” column counts those.
  • Triage has not been re-read since 2024 and is marked stale.

What the desk does now

  1. Keep #4471 for the parser problem only.
  2. Track the hint and one-day problems on their own tickets (linked in the table).
  3. New date reports: place them by tenant and channel (causeOf), not by subject, and read the body for a one-day move before merging.
